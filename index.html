About

Fashion should feel like a conversation, not a private club.

I'm a Nigerian-American fashion creator, stylist, and storyteller working between the DMV and New York. My work is rooted in the belief that you can't buy an eye — you build one through culture, experience, curiosity, and attention. I create fashion stories that help people dress with more intention, shop with more discernment, and develop a point of view of their own.

“You can't buy an eye. You build one.”
